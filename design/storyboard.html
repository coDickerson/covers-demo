Covers · working brief · draft for iteration

Check the dinner before the card works

The problem

Medical-device and pharma reps host speaker dinners for doctors every week. Every meal a doctor receives is a transfer of value: the company has to report it to the federal Open Payments database, and its own policy caps it (here: $125 per person per meal, $2,000 per doctor per year). The speaker must be approved and trained for the topic. Repeat invitations to the same doctor look like inducement.

Today those checks happen after the money is spent. The rep books the table, pays with the company card, and compliance reconstructs who ate what from receipts and sign-in sheets weeks later. By then a doctor may already be over her limit, a no-show may have been reported as a payment, and the wine is buried in "Beverages".

The card records that money moved and who was there. Nothing checks whether the dinner should happen.

Our solution

  • Before the dinner: the rep enters the plan. Covers identifies every guest in the federal NPI registry and checks the speaker, the per-person estimate and each doctor's running annual total. The Ramp card's meal budget unlocks only when every check passes.
  • After the dinner: one phone ping. The rep snaps the receipt and taps who actually came. Covers reads the receipt, splits the bill across the people who ate, updates each doctor's running total and drafts the Open Payments rows. No-shows get nothing reported.
  • Oversight: only the exceptions reach compliance, each with its evidence and the rule it came from. Policy changes are written in plain English, replayed over past dinners, and promoted by a person.
  • Trust: the model only reads (receipts, registry records). Deterministic code decides every amount and outcome, and every decision is cited and logged.

Design principles (from moa)

  • One question per screen, one visual per answer. Everything else is one click away in a side panel.
  • Zoom by levels: dinner → guest → evidence, with a breadcrumb.
  • Show progress as a chain of small cards that fill in: Plan → Checks → Card → Receipt → Settled.
  • Recommend, don't just report: decisions are offered as options with one marked Recommended.
  • Reward closure, not points: the stamp, "Settled", "Nothing left for you to do", "Audit trail intact".
